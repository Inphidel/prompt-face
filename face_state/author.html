Prompt

The face follows the expression and shifts left or right. Arms sit beside it, and a hand gesture can take that place. A hand on the face removes that arm and can use the cover face. A custom emote replaces the whole line.

On screen

The line updates when the tracker is connected.

The outlined face is the one on screen. Edit on screen opens it. The outlined field is the side in use while that face is selected. Click a character below to drop it into the field you are editing. Muted has the same left, center, and right fields. Face mute above only reports that state.

Both arms stay on screen. Down, or an arm the camera does not see, uses the down character, the shoulder. Up uses the up character. A hand on the face removes the arm on that side. A Stream Deck emote replaces the whole line until it is cleared.

Each hand has its own text. A matching hand shows that side, so one hand, both, or neither can be up. Clear a side and its arm character stays. The switches save on their own.

Each frame stays up for the hold, plus or minus a random amount.

Hats

A second OBS image, separate from Prompt. Put each picture in the images folder. Center and right show it as drawn. Looking left mirrors it by flipping the image's horizontal scale. The page stores the four pictures and reports the slot. The deck sends hat a, hat b, hat c, hat d, or hat none.
